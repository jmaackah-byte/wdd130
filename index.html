Accra

Accra is the vibrant capital city of Ghana, located along the Atlantic coast of West Africa. Known for its warm tropical weather, bustling local markets, and colorful coastline, it serves as the dynamic economic and cultural heart of the country. From historic landmarks and lively music to friendly neighborhoods and delicious local food, Accra offers a unique mix of rich traditions and modern city life.
